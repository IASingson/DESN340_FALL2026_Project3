Firing Services

Prices Vary

Bring us your kiln ready pieces. Firing costs are based on space used in the kiln when firing cone 4 and 6, other wise pricing is based on kiln load.

We offer several types of firing including:

  • Bisque (cone 04, electric)
  • Glaze (cone 6, electric)
  • Lustre (cone 018, electric) speciality
  • Wood firing (cone 10, wood) speciality
  • Salt + Soda (cone 10, gas) speciality
  • Raku (propane) speciality
